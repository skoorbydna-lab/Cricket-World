# Cricket World

A top-down cricket game for phones and desktops: swipe to bat and bowl, drag to field.

- **Career** – pick one of 12 nations, select your XI and play the T20 World Cup, 50-over World Cup and Test Championship. Players age, develop with form, retire and are replaced by youngsters.
- **Quick match** – any format, any two nations, with a choice of match length.
- **Practice nets** – batting, bowling and step-by-step tutorials.

All player names are fictional. Careers are saved in the browser (use the save code in the menu to back up).

Play: https://skoorbydna-lab.github.io/Cricket-World/
